# Executive Summary

RECOMMENDATION  
Move budget away from TV and into Search (and other underinvested channels), keeping total weekly spend the same at Rs.127,140.  
  
WHY  
TV has hit diminishing returns -- extra spend there barely increases conversions anymore. Search is still on the steep part of its response curve, so the same rupee produces more conversions there.  
  
IMPACT  
Projected uplift: +10.9% more conversions per week, worth an estimated Rs.12,861,360 in extra annual revenue (assuming Rs.1,200 value per conversion).
